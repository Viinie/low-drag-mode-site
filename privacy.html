Privacy Policy

Low Drag Mode Publisher · Last updated: October 7, 2026

Who we are

"Low Drag Mode Publisher" (the "App") is an internal tool operated by Vinicius Zeidler, owner of the Low Drag Mode channel (Brazil). Contact: lowdrag.mode@gmail.com.

Use of YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The App's use of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the channel owner, on his own computer, to manage his own YouTube channel. It is not available to the public and does not collect data from any other person.

What data the App accesses

  • Basic information about the owner's own YouTube channel (channel ID, title, handle) to confirm the correct account.
  • The owner's own videos, thumbnails and caption files, which the App uploads and updates on the owner's behalf.

The App does not access YouTube data of any other user or channel.

How the data is used, stored and shared

  • Data is used only to upload, schedule and update the owner's own videos.
  • The OAuth access token is stored only on the owner's computer, in a private folder, and is never sent to any third party.
  • No YouTube data is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics by third parties.
  • No cookies or tracking technologies are used by the App.

Retention and deletion

The stored access token is kept only while the App is in use. The owner can delete it at any time by removing the local token file. Access can also be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page, which immediately prevents the App from accessing the account.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
