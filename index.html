Low Drag Mode Publisher

Low Drag Mode Publisher is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the Low Drag Mode channel. It runs on the owner's own computer and uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own videos, set their thumbnails and captions, and schedule their publication. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
